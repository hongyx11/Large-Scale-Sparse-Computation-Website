Hi ! I'm Yuxi Hong, an assistant professor in the Department of Intelligent Systems Engineering at Indiana University Blo omington. My research focuses on large-scale sparse computation, numerical linear algebra, and high-performance computing. I am particularly interested in developing efficient algorithms for sparse matrix operations, as well as their applications in scientific computing and data analysis.
